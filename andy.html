01 // building

Useful systems for messy realities.

Thirty years in software, the last few spent on something that actually excites me: making LLMs do useful work in industries that run on messy documents and legacy systems.

I orchestrate multi-model AI pipelines that cut legal document processing by 75%. Before that: FinTech, InsurTech, cybersecurity, and telecom. The thread connecting all of it is taking complicated systems and making them talk to each other.

Think of it like jazz: you learn the theory so you can improvise. I’ve spent decades learning the theory. Now I get to improvise with LLMs.
